Does a wrapped vehicle pay for itself?

The short answer

Yes. Your trucks already drive past your customers — a wrap just makes it count. One wrapped vehicle draws up to 70,000 impressions a day in a busy metro, at about 35 cents per 1,000 views: the lowest cost per impression of any advertising medium. A quality wrap keeps selling for 5–7 years, so you pay once and advertise for the life of the vehicle.

By the numbers

  • Up to 70,000 impressions per day in a busy metro
  • ~35¢ per 1,000 views — lowest CPM of any medium
  • 5–7 year lifespan on a quality wrap
  • Up to 15x higher name recognition than other outdoor formats

Sources: OAAA / ARD outdoor advertising study; 3M vehicle-wrap study

One honest catch

It only works if the design earns the glance. At 40 mph a driver gets a two-second look — logo, one offer, one way to reach you.

Issue 02July 3, 20264 min readStorefront Signage

Will a new sign actually bring in customers?

The short answer

Yes — your sign is the best-value salesperson you will ever hire. 76% of people have entered a business for the first time simply because of its sign. Your storefront works 24/7, in front of every car and every pedestrian, whether or not you are open.

By the numbers

  • 76% have entered a business for the first time because of its sign
  • 68% judge product and service quality by signage
  • 60% say poor or missing signs kept them from going in
  • 50% say an unclear sign made a business hard to find

Sources: FedEx Office Signage Survey

One honest catch

A sign gets them to the door once. It brings them in; your brand brings them back.

Issue 03July 10, 20264 min readBrand Consistency

Does my brand have to look the same everywhere?

The short answer

Yes — and that consistency is worth up to 23% in revenue. A consistently presented brand sees an average revenue lift of 23%: same logo, same red, same type, from your truck to your business card to your trade-show booth. The repetition is what makes people remember you.

By the numbers

  • 23% average revenue lift from consistent presentation
  • 3.5x more brand visibility
  • 5–7 impressions before a buyer remembers you
  • 81% of consumers must trust a brand before they buy

Sources: Marq (Lucidpress) Brand Consistency Report; Edelman Trust Barometer

One honest catch

It only works when every piece matches. One off-brand flyer or a faded sign quietly undoes the work everything else is doing.

Issue 04July 17, 20264 min readTrade Shows

Are trade shows worth the investment?

The short answer

Yes, because the room is full of people who can say yes. Roughly four out of five attendees on a show floor have buying authority. Meeting a prospect at a show costs about $96; finding and meeting that same prospect in the field runs about $1,039 — roughly $943 saved on every handshake.

By the numbers

  • 81% of attendees have buying authority
  • $96 to meet a prospect at a show vs $1,039 in the field
  • 3.5 sales calls to close a show lead vs 4.5 from anywhere else

Sources: CEIR, Cost Effectiveness of Exhibition Participation; CEIR, The Spend Decision

One honest catch

The booth gets you noticed; the follow-up gets you business. Plan the follow-up before you order the backwall.

Issue 05July 24, 20264 min readPrint Marketing

Can better print make your business look more valuable?

The short answer

Yes. The finish tells people what you are worth before they read a word. Embellished pieces are 2.5x more attractive to consumers, and enhanced print is judged 46% higher quality than the same design run flat. Soft-touch laminate, spot UV, foil stamping, and sculpted emboss are the four finishes that make a piece feel premium in the hand.

By the numbers

  • 2.5x more attractive to consumers when embellished
  • 46% higher perceived quality than flat print
  • 21% less cognitive effort to process physical media
  • 75% could name the brand from a physical piece vs 44% from a digital ad
  • 16.8% response rate on an embellished postcard — about 31% higher

Sources: Foil & Specialty Effects Association; Clemson Sonoco Institute / FSEA; Canada Post / TrueImpact

One honest catch

Finish raises perceived value; it cannot rescue a weak offer. Get the message right first, then let the finish raise the ceiling.

Issue 06July 31, 20263 min readPromotional Products

Do branded promo products actually work?

The short answer

Yes — because people keep them, and remember you. 85% of consumers remember the advertiser who gave them a logoed product, and 78% keep a promotional product because they find it useful. Bags are the best performer; outerwear and drinkware are kept longest; pens are useful but discarded first.

By the numbers

  • 85% remember the advertiser who gave them a logoed product
  • 78% keep a promo product because they find it useful
  • 75% hold a positive opinion of branded merch
  • 76% are more likely to do business with a brand that gave them merch

Sources: 2026 ASI Global Advertising Impressions Study

One honest catch

It only works if it is worth keeping. A $1 pen used daily beats a $12 gadget nobody understands — buy fewer, better items.
