FAQ Friday Portal — Deployment & Maintenance Manual

How to publish new newsletters, reorder the library, compress images, and redeploy to Netlify.

YOU = steps you do yourself  ·  ASSISTANT = ask Claude to do it (needs the build tools)

00How the portal is built

The whole site is one folder: AG QR Portal/. You deploy that folder to Netlify. Inside it:

File / folderWhat it is
index.htmlThe landing page (service tiles, FAQ Friday list, contact card). This is a bundled file — all images and fonts are packed inside it.
newsletters/Week01.html …One self-contained newsletter per issue. Also bundled (~1 MB each).
doc-page.js, this manualSupport files. Harmless to leave in the folder; they don't affect the live site.

The editable sources live one level up, outside the deploy folder, and are what actually get edited:

SourcePurpose
FAQ Friday Newsletters/newsletter-data.jsAll newsletter copy, stats, captions, and slugs — the single source of truth for wording.
FAQ Friday Newsletters/newsletter-render.jsThe shared layout/renderer. Rarely changes.
FAQ Friday Newsletters/digital/WeekNN.htmlA tiny page that renders one week for preview before bundling.
assets/masters/ · assets/spotlight/The photos each newsletter uses.

The one rule that matters

Never hand-edit a file inside AG QR Portal/ in a text editor — they are compiled bundles. Always change the source and re-bundle. (The assistant handles the bundling; it also handles a subtle </script> escaping quirk that will silently break the page if edited by hand.)

01Publish a new newsletter (the weekly job)

Say the new one is Week 05. The pattern is identical every week — just change the number.

1 — Finalize the copy YOU

Confirm the wording for week 05 in newsletter-data.js reads the way you want. Check it against the voice rules (see §6). Make sure the week's photos exist in assets/masters/ and assets/spotlight/.

2 — Preview it ASSISTANT

Ask: "Show me the Week 05 digital newsletter." Read it top to bottom — headline, stats, spotlight captions, the "one honest catch," and the closing CTA.

3 — Bundle it into the deploy folder ASSISTANT

Ask: "Bundle Week 05 to the portal." Under the hood this makes a temp scaffold, runs the inliner, and writes AG QR Portal/newsletters/Week05.html. Result: a single ~1 MB self-contained file.

4 — Add it to the library list, at the top ASSISTANT

Ask: "Add Week 05 to the top of the FAQ Friday list on the portal." This inserts a new card with the issue number, category, date, and question, linking to ./newsletters/Week05.html. See §2 for exactly how the ordering works.

5 — Redeploy YOU

See §4. Download the folder and drop it into Netlify.

02Reorder the library (bump older issues down)

The FAQ Friday list shows the two newest issues up top and hides the rest behind an "Earlier issues" expander. Newest is always first; oldest is last.

When a new issue is added, everything shifts down by one:

  • The new issue becomes visible #1.
  • The previous #1 becomes visible #2.
  • The previous #2 drops into the "Earlier issues" section, at the top of that hidden list.
  • Everything already hidden stays in order below it.

In plain terms: "newest two shown, all older ones collapsed, newest-first throughout." Just tell the assistant "bump the list — newest two visible, the rest under Earlier issues" and it will re-sort the cards. You never have to touch the code.

Each card needs

Issue number, category (e.g. Trade Shows), publish date, the question headline, and the link to its WeekNN.html. Give these to the assistant and it builds the card.

03Compress images (keep the site fast & cheap)

Image weight is the #1 driver of Netlify bandwidth cost. Two places to watch:

Portal landing page tiles

The five service-tile photos are baked into index.html. Target: JPEG, ≤ 1200 px on the long edge, quality ~0.82, ≤ ~250 KB each. The whole landing page should stay under ~1.5 MB (it was 9.2 MB before compression — that one fix multiplied the free-tier headroom ~6×).

Newsletter photos

Masters and spotlights in assets/. Spotlights are square (1:1); masters are the wide hero image. A finished newsletter around ~1 MB is healthy.

To compress, ask the assistant: "Compress the portal tile images" or "these photos are too heavy — shrink them." It decodes each image, resizes, and re-encodes as JPEG in place. ASSISTANT

Quick sanity check

A full visit (landing page + one newsletter) should total roughly 2–3 MB. On Netlify's free allowance that's several thousand visits a month — plenty for QR-scan traffic.

04Deploy to Netlify

  1. Ask the assistant: "Give me the portal folder to deploy." You'll get a downloadable AG QR Portal zip. ASSISTANT
  2. Unzip it. YOU
  3. First time: at app.netlify.com → Add new site → Deploy manually, then drag the unzipped folder onto the drop area. Netlify gives you a URL. YOU
  4. Updates: open your site → Deploys tab → drag the new folder onto the drop zone. It replaces the live version in seconds. YOU

Deploy the folder, not just index.html

The landing page links to ./newsletters/Week01.html etc. If you upload only index.html, every newsletter link 404s. Always deploy the whole folder.

Free-tier notes

  • New Netlify accounts: ~15 GB bandwidth/month (credit model). Accounts created before Sep 2025 keep the older ~100 GB.
  • The free plan doesn't auto-bill — if you exhaust it, the site simply pauses until the next month. No surprise charges.
  • Only consider upgrading (Personal $9 / Pro $20) if scans climb into the tens of thousands per month. Cloudflare Pages is a free alternative with unlimited bandwidth.

05The "Save Our Contact Info" logo

The Save button downloads a contact card (vCard) with the AG logo embedded, so it becomes the contact photo on a phone. If the logo ever changes, ask the assistant to "re-embed the new logo in the contact card." It re-encodes a square ~400 px JPEG into the card. ASSISTANT

Testing tip

iPhones cache contacts and web pages aggressively. When testing a change, delete the old saved contact first, and hard-refresh the page, or the old version can linger.

06Voice & tone guardrails

Every newsletter must sound like a partner, not a salesperson. The hard rules:

  • Never the word "cheap" — use affordable or best-value.
  • No transactional lines like "we sell X" or "we're telling you Y." Lead with wanting to help, then be candid.
  • Don't overclaim in-house production for outsourced items (trade-show displays, large-format). It's fine to say local produce/print/install for things AG genuinely runs locally.

Full reference: FAQ Friday Newsletters/FAQ Friday — Voice & Tone Guide.html.
